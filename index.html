Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I restore my previous purchase on a new device?

Open the app settings or paywall screen and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase, your Pro access will be restored immediately without any charge.

Q: Are your apps subscription-based or one-time purchases?

Our applications prioritize a clean, one-time lifetime purchase (Buy Once, Own Forever) model with a free trial period. No recurring monthly or annual subscription fees.

Q: How can I request a refund?

All transactions, billing, and refunds are managed directly by Apple. If you wish to request a refund, please visit Apple's official portal at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Q: Found a bug or have a feature request?

Please email us at genkanaiduka@gmail.com with your device model, macOS / iOS version, and a brief description of the issue. We review and respond to feedback actively.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all software applications and services developed and distributed by GEN Kanaizuka through the Apple App Store. Your privacy is paramount, and our software is designed strictly with a privacy-first approach.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe your personal data belongs to you. Our applications:

  • Do not require user account registration.
  • Do not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal identifying information (PII).
  • Do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking networks.
  • Do not access your camera, microphone, photos, or files without your explicit, on-demand permission.

2. Local Processing & File Access

All data processing—including media analysis, checksum calculation, timeline cues, audio handling, and cataloging—is performed strictly on your local device. Your files, media clips, and production documents never leave your hardware.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

All financial transactions and in-app purchases are securely handled exclusively by Apple via StoreKit. We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

4. Crash Reports and Diagnostics

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers through Apple's operating system settings, we may receive anonymized crash logs and basic system diagnostic information solely to debug and improve app stability.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
